# MELSAVA — Account & Data Deletion Policy

**Developer:** TechnOrchid  
**Application:** MELSAVA  
**Contact Email:** technorchid.dev@gmail.com  
**Last Updated:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Overview
At MELSAVA (developed by **TechnOrchid**), we respect your privacy and provide complete control over your personal data. You have the right to permanently delete your MELSAVA account and all associated personal data at any time.

---

## 2. How to Delete Your Account

### Method 1: Directly Inside the MELSAVA App (Instant)
You can permanently delete your account directly from within the app without contacting customer support:
1. Open the **MELSAVA** app on your device.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Tap the **Settings** gear icon in the top right.
4. Scroll to the **Account & Safety** section and select **Delete Account**.
5. Re-authenticate your identity (enter your current password).
6. Type **DELETE** in the confirmation box to confirm your choice.
7. Tap **Permanently Delete My Account**.

Your session will end immediately, and your account and data will be permanently purged from our servers.

---

### Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request (For Uninstalled Apps)
If you have uninstalled the app, lost device access, or prefer to request deletion via the web:
1. Send an email to **technorchid.dev@gmail.com** from the email address registered with your MELSAVA account.
2. Use the subject line: **`MELSAVA - Account Deletion Request`**.
3. Include your registered email address and display name in the body of the email.
4. Our team will verify ownership and complete the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data within **48 hours**, sending you a final confirmation email.

---

## 3. What Data is Deleted?
When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently erased from our production databases:
- **Profile Information:** Your name, email, display name, age, city, bio, and interests.
- **Photos & Media:** Your profile photos and avatars stored in cloud storage.
- **Messages & Conversations:** All your 1-on-1 private chat messages and conversation records.
- **Connections & Matches:** All mutual connection records, connection requests, and match history.
- **Activity Attendance:** Your attendance history and RSVP records for past or upcoming circles/experiences.
- **Authentication & Security Data:** Passwords, OAuth links, session tokens, and device identifiers.

---

## 4. Data Retention Policy
- **Instant Deletion:** All personal identifiers, profile pictures, and messages are deleted immediately upon account deletion confirmation.
- **Safety & Legal Retention Exception:** If an account was subject to an active safety report, harassment investigation, or financial fraud inquiry prior to deletion, minimum non-identifying moderation logs may be retained in secure restricted storage for up to **90 days** solely to comply with legal safety regulations and protect community members. After 90 days, these logs are permanently purged.
- **Backups:** Automated database disaster-recovery backups overwrite and purge deleted records on a rolling 30-day lifecycle.
