## Zero-Tolerance Mandate
TechnOrchid strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), grooming, solicitation of minors, or child endangerment within the MELSAVA platform. Any violation results in immediate permanent account termination, forensic preservation, and mandatory criminal reporting to national and international law enforcement bodies.

---

## 1. Strict Age Restriction (18+ Adults Only)
MELSAVA is an activity-first social discovery and dating application designed exclusively for consenting adults aged 18 and older. Minors under the age of 18 are legally and contractually prohibited from creating accounts, accessing the app, or participating in any community circles.
- Age declarations are mandatory during account registration.
- Any accounts found or suspected to belong to minors under the age of 18 are suspended immediately.
- Associated data is isolated and purged in accordance with our data protection policies unless required for law enforcement handover.

---

## 2. Prevention, Detection & Content Moderation
TechnOrchid employs multi-layered technical, procedural, and community safeguards to prevent, detect, and neutralize prohibited content:
- **Text & Chat Monitoring:** Proactive automated scanning of profile text, bios, and messaging metadata for keywords associated with minor exploitation, grooming, and child endangerment.
- **Media Screening:** Automated image moderation filters and hash-matching algorithms that block known abusive imagery before upload.
- **In-App User Reporting:** Every profile, photo, and direct chat includes an instant, 1-tap **Report** button with designated safety categories. Reports involving minor safety or potential exploitation bypass normal queues and are prioritized for immediate review within minutes.
- **Instant Blocking:** Users can instantly block any individual, severing all messaging and discovery visibility immediately.

---

## 3. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC
In compliance with global laws and statutory reporting obligations (including 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and regional child protection legislation):
- **NCMEC CyberTipline:** TechnOrchid reports all detected instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and online exploitation of children to the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** via the CyberTipline.
- **Law Enforcement Cooperation:** We actively coordinate with domestic and international law enforcement agencies, preserving relevant technical logs, IP records, account information, and evidentiary payloads to aid in the apprehension and prosecution of offenders.
