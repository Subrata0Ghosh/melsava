Privacy Policy for MELSAVA

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Publisher: TechnOrchid ("we", "our", or "us")

1. Introduction

MELSAVA is an activity-first social connection application designed to help adults meet through real-world shared experiences. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitments regarding the collection, use, and protection of your personal information.

2. Adult Eligibility (18+)

MELSAVA is exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

3. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address and authentication credentials.
  • Public Profile: Display name, profile photo, bio, broad area/city, interests, and social intentions.
  • Experience Activity: Activities joined, attendee attendance, and group participation.
  • Private Post-Experience Choices: Your private selections of people you wish to reconnect with are strictly confidential and are NEVER shared unless both parties make a compatible mutual choice.

4. How We Use Information

  • To discover and coordinate small-group real-world experiences.
  • To process mutual connections using server-side atomic matching.
  • To maintain community safety, enforce guidelines, and handle reports/blocks.

5. Account Deletion & Rights

You may request full account deletion at any time within the app settings or by contacting support@technorchid.com. Upon deletion, all personal profile data, private choices, and messages are permanently purged.

6. Contact Us

For questions or data requests, contact TechnOrchid at support@technorchid.com.
