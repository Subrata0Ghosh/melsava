Privacy Policy for MELSAVA

Effective Date: November 18, 2026  ·  Publisher: TechnOrchid ("we", "our", or "us")

1. Introduction

MELSAVA is an activity-first social connection application designed to help adults meet through real-world shared experiences. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

2. Adult Eligibility (18+)

MELSAVA is strictly for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we promptly delete it.

3. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information: Email address, authentication credentials, and—when you choose Sign in with Google—your basic Google profile information (display name, email address, and profile photo URL). We only request basic identity scopes (email and profile) and never access your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Calendar.
  • Profile Details: Display name, avatar, bio, city/area, interests, and social intent preferences you choose to share.
  • Location Information (Optional): With your explicit permission, approximate or device GPS coordinates are used to show nearby activities and public meetup venues on the map. You can also search and select any city or venue manually without enabling GPS.
  • Experience & Messaging Activity: Activities you RSVP to, micro-group attendance, and messages exchanged with mutual connections.
  • Confidential Post-Experience Reconnect Choices: Selections of people you wish to reconnect with after an experience are strictly private and are never disclosed unless both participants make a compatible mutual choice.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account securely via Email or Google Sign-In.
  • To display nearby experiences, local venues, and global map locations.
  • To coordinate safe small-group meetups and process private mutual matches.
  • To enforce Community Safety Standards, prevent abuse, and respond to user reports or support requests.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We rely on trusted infrastructure providers strictly to operate MELSAVA:

  • Supabase: Secure user authentication and backend data storage with encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Google Sign-In: Optional OAuth 2.0 authentication if you choose to continue with Google.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Overpass: Public map tiles and place/venue search queries.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account data only while your account is active or as required by law. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time from inside the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by emailing technorchid.dev@gmail.com with the subject line "MELSAVA Account Deletion Request". Upon confirmation, your profile, messages, and private choices are permanently deleted.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), scoped OAuth tokens, and strict access controls to safeguard your data.

8. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, data export, or account deletion requests, contact TechnOrchid at technorchid.dev@gmail.com.
