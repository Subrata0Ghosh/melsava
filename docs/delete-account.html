Account & Data Deletion Request

App: MELSAVA  ·  Developer: TechnOrchid  ·  Contact: technorchid.dev@gmail.com  ·  Updated: November 2026

At MELSAVA (developed by TechnOrchid), we are committed to transparent data management. You have the absolute right to delete your account, your profile, and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the MELSAVA app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the MELSAVA app.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top right corner.
  4. Scroll down to the Account & Safety section and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your identity by entering your current password.
  6. Type DELETE in the confirmation box.
  7. Tap Permanently Delete My Account.

✓ This immediately wipes your user session, removes your profile from discovery, and triggers backend data deletion.

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request (For Uninstalled Apps)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot log in to your account:

  1. Send an email to our official developer support at technorchid.dev@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: MELSAVA - Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the email from the same email address associated with your MELSAVA account.
  4. Include your display name and registered email in the email body so we can locate your record.
Submit Email Deletion Request

Our security team will verify ownership and permanently delete your account and associated data within 48 hours, followed by a written confirmation.

What Types of Data Are Deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data categories are permanently removed:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, display name, date of birth / age, city / location coordinates, and bio.
  • Photos and Media: Profile photos, uploaded avatars, and any image files in cloud storage.
  • Messages & Communications: All private direct chat messages, icebreakers, and mutual connection chat transcripts.
  • Connections & Matches: Sent, received, and mutual connection records, along with match history.
  • Activity Participation: RSVP status, circle attendance records, and past activity participation logs.
  • Authentication & Security Credentials: Encrypted password hashes, OAuth tokens, session tokens, and device identifiers.

Data Retention Policy

  • Immediate Purge: Profile data, media files, and messages are wiped immediately upon confirmed deletion.
  • Safety & Legal Retention Exception: In compliance with legal regulations and community safety standards, if an account was subject to an active safety report, harassment complaint, or illegal activity investigation prior to the deletion request, minimal non-identifying moderation logs may be retained in restricted storage for up to 90 days before permanent deletion.
  • System Backups: Secure encrypted database disaster-recovery backups overwrite deleted records within a rolling 30-day lifecycle.

Permanent and Irreversible Action

Deleting your MELSAVA account cannot be undone. All active connections, mutual chats, and community trust badges will be permanently lost.
