# MELSAVA — Product Overview

## Vision

A world where people connect through shared experiences, not superficial profiles. MELSAVA reimagines social discovery by putting real moments at the center of every connection.

## Mission

To build a platform where adults can discover activities they genuinely enjoy, meet compatible people through those activities, and form meaningful connections — whether friendship, activity partnerships, or romantic relationships — based on mutual interest and shared experiences.

---

## Target Market

### Primary Market
**India** — Starting with one city or concentrated community, then expanding to additional cities before going international.

### Target Audience
- Adults aged **18 and above**
- No arbitrary upper age limit
- People seeking genuine social connections beyond traditional swipe-based platforms
- Individuals interested in activity-based socializing
- People who value shared experiences over profile browsing

### Launch Strategy
1. Start with **one city** or concentrated community
2. Seed curated experiences with approved hosts
3. Invite a small adult community for closed beta
4. Collect feedback and iterate
5. Expand to additional cities

## Core Product Loop

```
DISCOVER → JOIN → PARTICIPATE → CHOOSE → CONNECT
```

| Step | Description |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | Users browse curated experiences and activities they genuinely want to participate in |
| **Join** | Users RSVP to a small-group experience (3–5 participants) |
| **Participate** | Users attend the activity, interact naturally, and enjoy the experience |
| **Choose** | After the activity, each participant privately selects who they'd like to reconnect with |
| **Connect** | When selections are mutual, a connection is created and a private conversation opens |

This loop replaces the traditional swipe → match → message model with something grounded in real interaction.

---

## Key Differentiator: Post-Experience Mutual Connection

The most important feature of MELSAVA is the **post-experience mutual connection system**.

### How It Works

1. A small group (e.g., 4 people) attends a curated activity together
2. After the activity, each participant sees a private selection screen
3. For each fellow participant, they choose one of:
   - **Friendship** — Connect as friends
   - **Activity Partner** — Connect for future activities together
   - **Explore Dating** — Explore a romantic connection
   - **No Connection** — Pass without consequence
4. All selections are completely private
5. The system compares each pair of participants:
   - If **both** people select each other with compatible intentions → **Connection created**
   - If only one person selects the other → **Nothing happens**
6. A private conversation opens for each new connection
7. **Rejections are never revealed** — no notifications, no indication

### Why This Matters

- Connections are grounded in real interaction, not just photos
- Mutual consent is built into the core mechanism
- There is no way to purchase, unlock, or force another person's consent
- The privacy of choices eliminates the fear of rejection
- A friendship selection never automatically becomes a romantic match

## Connection Intentions

MELSAVA supports **seven connection intentions** that users can select (multiple allowed, one primary):

| # | Intention | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Genuine Friendship** | Looking for real friendships |
| 2 | **Activity Partner** | Someone to share hobbies and activities with |
| 3 | **Casual Dating** | Open to casual romantic connections |
| 4 | **Meaningful Dating** | Looking for deeper romantic connections |
| 5 | **Serious Relationship** | Seeking a committed relationship |
| 6 | **Marriage-Minded** | Open to connections that could lead to marriage |
| 7 | **Open to Discovering** | No specific expectation — open to whatever develops |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Friendship is a complete and valuable use case. Not all users are seeking romance, and the platform must serve friendship-seekers equally well.

## Safety Principles

Safety is not an afterthought — it's a core requirement from the first release.

### Built-In Safeguards

- **Adult eligibility gate** before any social interaction
- **Terms of Service & Community Guidelines** acceptance required
- **Profile reporting** and **message reporting**
- **User blocking** with full interaction prevention
- **Account suspension** and **moderation** support
- **Account deletion** available to all users
- **Public venues only** for in-person activities
- **No live location tracking**
- **No private address exposure**
- **Host approval** required before experience creation
- **Safe meeting guidance** provided to participants

### Privacy Protections

- Post-experience selections are always private
- Exact date of birth is never publicly exposed
- Private profile data is separated from public discovery data
- Users control what information they share
- Blocked users cannot rejoin through alternate flows
